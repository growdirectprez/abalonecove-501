Anonymous contribute form

Tell us what you know

Submissions are anonymous. Reviewed by two editors. Not published unless verified.

How this works — read first if you have not already. Short version: no IP logs, no cookies, no analytics, encrypted at rest, two-editor review, rebuttal offered if anything published names you or your property.

This form is a stub

The backend is not yet deployed. Submitting will show you a confirmation but will not transmit the submission anywhere. When the Cloudflare Worker at contribute.abalonecove.org is live, this form will begin encrypting submissions and routing them to the editor queue. Until then, use email at the address on the About page for urgent matters.

What kind of contribution is this?

File upload is disabled until the Worker is live; the handler for media uploads requires R2 pre-signed URLs that don't exist yet.

Leave blank to stay fully anonymous. If you provide a handle, the editors may reach out once; they will not re-contact after that without your explicit reply.

What happens next: when the backend is live, your submission will be encrypted in your browser with an age public key before transmission. Only the two named editors can decrypt it. Unverified submissions are not published. If an editor wants to follow up on a claim, and you left a contact, they will reach out once. You may reply, or not.

Questions about what the position paper says, or a rebuttal to something published here, go through the same form — use the historical knowledge category and mention which passage you're responding to.

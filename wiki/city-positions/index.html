City Positions Cross-Reference

This article tracks what the City of Rancho Palos Verdes has formally stated about key issues across different documents, meetings, and legal filings. The purpose is to find inconsistencies, contradictions, and evolving positions that can be used in litigation and community advocacy.

How to use this: When a new city document is captured, scan it for claims on these topics and add them here with date, source, and the specific statement. Contradictions between entries on the same topic are the gold.

Topic 1: Density & Rezoning of 0 Clipper

City's position (Demurrer, Nov 2024): The City rezoned 0 Clipper from RS-4 to RM-22 as part of Housing Element compliance. Three ordinances passed: 678U (April 16, 2024, urgency), 680U (June 4, 2024, urgency), 681 (June 18, 2024, regular). All contain the same amendments. Source: City Demurrer

City's implied position: Density calculation based on 1.56 acres (full parcel). Source: Housing Element Site #16 designation.

Our position: Assessor's own map shows usable lot is ~1.01 acres (flood hazard zone reduces it). Actual density is ~16 units/acre, not ~10. Nobody checked county records before rezoning. Source: 0 Clipper Road article

Contradiction to watch: HCD confirmed in January 2025 that the city can remove Site 16 and remain RHNA-compliant. City Council voted to keep it anyway in June 2025. Why keep a site you don't need in an extreme geological hazard area?

Topic 2: Mootness & Ordinance Supersession

City's position (Demurrer, Nov 2024): Even if Court invalidates Ordinance 681, Ordinances 678U and 680U remain in effect with the same zoning. Petition is moot. Cites RPV Municipal Code § 1.01.080. Source: City Demurrer

Our position (Opposition, Dec 2024): "Supersede" means "to annul, make void, or repeal" (Black's Law Dictionary). 681 superseded 680U which superseded 678U. Only 681 is operative. § 1.01.080 addresses invalidation of the entire Code, not individual ordinances — if anything, it supports reverting to R-4. Urgency ordinances are temporary measures, not replacements for regular ordinances. Source: Opposition to Demurrer

City's internal contradiction: The City itself admits Ordinance 681 "superseded" the urgency ordinances (Demurrer language). Yet argues the superseded ordinances still have independent legal effect. Can't have it both ways.

Topic 3: Coastal Act Applicability

City's position (Demurrer, Nov 2024): Legislative rezoning is not "development" under the Coastal Act (PRC § 30106). Even if it is, the rezoning isn't effective pending Coastal Commission certification (PRC § 30514(a)). City submitted amendments to CCC for certification via Resolution 2024-17. Source: City Demurrer

Our position (Opposition, Dec 2024): "Development" under the Coastal Act receives "expansive interpretation" per Pacific Palisades Bowl Mobile Estates v. City of LA (55 Cal.4th 783, 796). Definition explicitly includes "change in the density or intensity of use of land." Upzoning from RS-4 to RM-22 is a 5x density increase. City admits no case law holds rezoning is NOT development. Source: Opposition to Demurrer

City's contradiction: Argues the petition was filed too LATE for the urgency ordinances AND too EARLY for Coastal Act purposes (not yet certified). The petitioners are simultaneously late and early.

Topic 4: Geological Hazards

City's own documents (Zone 2 Landslide EIR): Residential plumbing systems were "likely catalysts" for slide movement. Groundwater is "the controlling factor." Source: Zone 2 EIR Geology Section

City's actions (Landslide Working Group, May 2025): Ground is moving 2.14 inches per week. Abalone Cove Landslide Abatement District operates 22 dewatering wells. Governor declared State of Emergency. Source: Working Group Meeting Notes

City's contradiction with rezoning: The city documents extreme geological hazard in the Portuguese Bend area AND proposes to increase density in the same area. Adding 16 units of plumbing in an area where "residential plumbing systems were likely catalysts" for landslide movement.

Topic 5: Housing Element & Builder's Remedy

City's position (Demurrer, Nov 2024): City was required to adopt Housing Element per Gov. Code § 65580. Missed October 15, 2022 deadline. Subject to Builder's Remedy penalties until Housing Element adopted and sites rezoned. Source: City Demurrer

HCD's position (January 2025): City can remove Site 16 and remain RHNA-compliant. The city does NOT need this site.

City Council action (June 2025): Voted to keep Site 16 anyway.

Question to press: If the Builder's Remedy threat justified the original urgency, and HCD says the site isn't needed, what justifies keeping it? Follow the money — Clipper Development LLC (Ali Vahdani) political donations to RPV campaigns.

Topic 6: CC&Rs & Private Restrictions

City's position: Not addressed in demurrer (the litigation attacks government process, not private rights).

Our position: Separate legal track. The Lot H Declaration (1950) imposes single-family restrictions that run with the land. No termination agreement has ever been recorded. Auto-renews every 10 years. Currently in Renewal 6 through January 1, 2035. Source: Lot H Discovery, Cove Legal Framework

Status: THEORY → needs confirmation. Key question: does Lot H actually cover APN 7573-006-024? Title search needed. See Legal Framework risk assessment.

Topic 7: Political Reform Act / Conflict of Interest

Our position (Petition, Sept 2024): Fourth cause of action — Political Reform Act violation and common law conflict of interest. Source: Petition Filed

Supporting evidence: FPPC Complaint filed against Cruikshank (former Mayor). Source: FPPC Complaint

City's response: Not specifically addressed in demurrer beyond general demurrer to all causes.

How to Add New Entries

When processing a new city document (council meeting minutes, staff report, public hearing transcript):

  1. Read the document for claims on any of the above topics
  2. Add the claim under the relevant topic heading with: date, source wikilink, and the specific statement
  3. If the claim contradicts an existing entry, note it explicitly
  4. If it's a new topic, create a new section following the same format
  5. Update the needs-review date in frontmatter to 30 days from now

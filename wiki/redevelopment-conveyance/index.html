RPV Redevelopment Agency Conveyance (2014)

On November 4, 2014, the Successor Agency to the Rancho Palos Verdes Redevelopment Agency conveyed 11 governmental-use properties to the City of RPV via grant deed, pursuant to Resolution No. SA 2014-__. This was the disposition mechanism under the agency's Long Range Property Management Plan (LRPMP).

Legal Authority Chain

  1. AB X1 26 (June 2011) — dissolved California redevelopment agencies
  2. California Redevelopment Association v. Matosantos (53 Cal. 4th 231, 2011) — upheld dissolution
  3. RPV Redevelopment Agency dissolved February 1, 2012; Successor Agency and Oversight Board established
  4. AB 1484 (June 2012) — supplemented dissolution act (together with AB X1 26, the "Dissolution Act")
  5. HSC § 34175(b) — all real properties transferred to Successor Agency by operation of law
  6. HSC § 34191.5(b) — Successor Agency required to prepare LRPMP for property disposition
  7. Oversight Board Resolution OB-2013-08 (Sept 18, 2013) — approved the LRPMP
  8. CA Dept of Finance letter dated April 25, 2014 — DOF did not initiate a review (deemed approved by inaction)
  9. HSC § 34191.3 — DOF-approved LRPMP governs and supersedes all other Dissolution Act provisions re: property

Key Finding: DOF Did Not Independently Review the LRPMP

The September 9, 2015 Oversight Board minutes confirm that the DOF did not initiate an independent review of the LRPMP. Under HSC § 34191.5(b), if the DOF does not act within a specified period, the LRPMP is deemed approved. The April 25, 2014 letter likely confirms this deemed-approved status rather than an affirmative review.

RPV Redevelopment Project Area

RPV Redevelopment Project Area No. 1 covered the Abalone Cove, Portuguese Bend, and Klondike Canyon landslide areas — corresponding to the ancient landslide complex and the city's landslide moratorium area. All 11 conveyed properties fall within this project area.

CRITICAL FINDING: Shoreline Park Parcels Are Declaration 101 Land

Verified 2026-04-17. The five Shoreline Park parcels conveyed in 2014 are subdivisions of the same land described in Declaration No. 101 (Book 9482, Page 37, 1929).

Parcel Correspondence

The Shoreline Park parcels (APNs 7572-018-900, 7573-007-900, 7572-019-900, 7572-019-901) are coastal parcels running from the PV Drive South right-of-way to Mean High Tide Line. They correspond to portions of:

  • Declaration 101, Parcel 1 (36.50 acres) — coastal strip from PV Coast Road to Mean High Tide Line
  • Declaration 101, Parcel 5 (43.10 acres) — coastal strip between road and ocean, SE of Parcel 2, to Mean High Tide Line

Both Declaration 101 parcels and the 2014 Shoreline Park parcels share the same parent (Lot H, Case 2373), the same coastal boundary (Mean High Tide Line), and the same corporate deed chain (PV Corp → Filiorum Corp).

Implication

When the city accepted these parcels in 2014 "subject to all liens, encumbrances, easements, covenants, conditions, and restrictions of record," it accepted land that traces directly to the parcels described in Declaration 101. Declaration 100 (Book 9436, p155) established Art Jury and protective restrictions for this land. The 1930 Filiorum grant deed (Book 10226, p170) expressly incorporated every provision of Declarations 100 and 101. These recorded instruments were never referenced in the 2014 conveyance memo, the LRPMP, or any city council discussion.

Other Relevance to 0 Clipper / Cove Map Module

  • APN 7573-007-900 is one of the conveyed Shoreline Park parcels — same assessor book (7573) as the 0 Clipper parcel (7573-006-024). These are adjacent in the assessor grid.
  • The Archery Range easement language (Portuguese Bend Landslide stabilization) documents the city's awareness of active landslide conditions in the immediate vicinity.
  • All Cherryhill Lane parcels are in Tract 14118, a separate subdivision from the WPBCA area (Tract 14649/43725).
  • The RPV Redevelopment Project Area No. 1 covered the entire landslide complex area — understanding which other parcels the RDA held (and how they were disposed) could reveal additional city-owned land near the WPBCA boundary.

LRPMP — Status and Next Steps

The actual LRPMP document is not available online. Neither the RPV city website nor the DOF website has it posted. To obtain it:

  1. PRA request to City of RPV Finance Department — for the LRPMP document, Resolution OB-2013-08, and the DOF determination letter
  2. Email DOF at RedevelopmentAdministration@dof.ca.gov — request the April 25, 2014 determination letter for Rancho Palos Verdes (pre-2020 letters only available by request)
  3. Check RPV FY2014-15 CAFR — Note #14 to financial statements covers the Successor Agency fund and may list transferred properties
